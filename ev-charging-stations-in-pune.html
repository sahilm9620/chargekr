EV Charging Stations in Pune

Find the best EV charging stations in Pune, from fast chargers on highway routes to convenient city and shared chargers.

Pune charging hotspots

Pune is building a strong EV charging network across Hinjewadi, Magarpatta, Kalyani Nagar, Baner, and the Mumbai-Pune Expressway corridor. These areas now host both public and private charging locations.

Fast chargers in Pune

For quick top-ups, focus on DC fast chargers and high-power AC chargers. Search for stations near business parks, malls, and highway rest stops to minimize waiting time.

Shared chargers and peer-to-peer options

ChargeKr makes it easy to discover shared chargers from homes and businesses in Pune. These chargers can be useful when public networks are busy or when you need a convenient city charging option.

How to choose a Pune charger

Check connector compatibility: Ensure the charger matches your EV’s connector.
Read reviews: Pick chargers with good feedback for reliability and safety.
Reserve when possible: Booking ahead reduces uncertainty during peak hours.
