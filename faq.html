Help & Support

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about finding, reserving, and hosting chargers on the ChargeKr community network.

What is ChargeKr?
ChargeKr is an Indian peer-to-peer technology platform that connects electric vehicle (EV) drivers with independent charger hosts offering parking and charging point access. ChargeKr provides charger discovery, scheduling, navigation, and payment facilitation tools.
How does finding and booking a charger work?
Drivers use the ChargeKr app to discover available chargers by location and connector type (15A socket, Type-2 AC wallbox). Select an available slot, confirm booking details, and navigate directly to the host's parking bay.
What is the difference between Instant Booking and Host Approval?
Instant Booking: The host has enabled automated reservations; your slot is instantly confirmed upon booking.
Host Approval: The host receives your request and can review and approve it based on spot availability before the reservation is finalised.
How are charging sessions priced and paid?
Hosts set transparent hourly reservation fees for parking spot and charger availability (typically ₹30 to ₹60 per hour). Payment is settled securely within the app using UPI, cards, or net banking. ChargeKr does not meter, sell, or resell units of electricity.
What electrical safety standards must a host charger meet?
Hosts must ensure that all charging equipment is installed by a certified licensed electrician. The circuit must include proper copper earthing, a dedicated Miniature Circuit Breaker (MCB), and a 30mA Residual Current Circuit Breaker (RCCB) complying with Central Electricity Authority (CEA) safety guidelines.
What is the cancellation and refund policy?
Drivers can cancel reservations free of charge up to 30 minutes before the scheduled booking time. If a host cancels or if the parking spot is inaccessible upon arrival, a full 100% refund is initiated back to your original payment method within 5 to 7 business days.
Can I list my charger if I live in an apartment society (CHS / RWA)?
Yes, provided that your housing society bye-laws and parking management allow guest vehicle access. Charger owners are responsible for obtaining any necessary society or RWA approvals for guest parking and access prior to listing.
Who is responsible for electricity connection and DISCOM compliance?
The charger owner (host) is solely responsible for ensuring their electrical setup adheres to state DISCOM regulations, sanctioned load limits, and applicable tariff schedules. ChargeKr functions strictly as a technology intermediary.
How do I reach customer support or escalate a grievance?
You can contact our support team at info@chargekr.com or support@chargekr.com. Under the Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules 2020, our Grievance Officer acknowledges formal complaints within 48 hours and provides resolution within 30 days.
Where can I download the official ChargeKr app?
ChargeKr is available for Android on the Google Play Store (Package: com.chargekr) and for iOS on the Apple App Store.

Have more questions?

Our team is here to assist you with any questions regarding hosting, charging, or account management.
