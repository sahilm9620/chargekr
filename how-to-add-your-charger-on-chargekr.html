How to Add Your Charger on ChargeKr

Learn how to list your EV charger, set availability, and earn money by sharing it with nearby drivers.

Step 1: Register and verify

Create a ChargeKr account, complete the profile, and verify your identity. Verification helps build trust for drivers who want to book your charger.

Step 2: Add charger details

Enter your charger type, connector type, power rating, and location. Upload clear photos and share any access instructions so drivers can find and use your charger easily.

Step 3: Set pricing and availability

Choose a competitive per-kWh or hourly rate, and set the hours when your charger is available. You can block off times when the charger is reserved for personal use.

Step 4: Start receiving bookings

Once your charger is live, nearby EV drivers can find it, book a slot, and pay through the app. ChargeKr handles the booking process so you can focus on hosting.
