01 HARDWARE SPECIFICATIONS • INDIA STANDARDS

EV Charger Types.
Engineered for Every Port.

From a residential 15A smart socket in an apartment stilt bay to a 22 kW three-phase Type-2 wallbox learn how power output, connectors, and charging speeds match your electric vehicle.

01 / CHARGING TIERS

Three architectures. One unified network.

TIER 01 // AC SLOW 230V SINGLE PHASE

Level 1 · 15A Smart Socket

Built on India's standard 230V 15A domestic power point. Ideal for electric scooters (Ather, Ola, TVS iQube) and overnight top-ups for compact electric cars in residential parking bays.

Power Output 2.3 kW – 3.3 kW
Range Added +18 to 25 km / hour
Connector IS 1293 15A 3-Pin / Portable EVSE
Typical Host Tariff ₹25 – ₹35 / hour
TIER 02 // AC WALLBOX ● MOST POPULAR ON CHARGEKR

Level 2 · Type-2 AC Wallbox

Dedicated 7.4 kW (single-phase) or 11–22 kW (three-phase) home and workplace wallboxes. Delivers a full 0–100% battery charge in 4 to 6 hours with zero battery thermal stress.

Power Output 7.4 kW – 22 kW AC
Range Added +45 to 110 km / hour
Connector Type 2 (IEC 62196-2 Mennekes)
Typical Host Tariff ₹45 – ₹80 / hour
TIER 03 // DC RAPID 400V–800V DIRECT CURRENT

Level 3 · CCS2 DC Fast

High-voltage direct current stations installed at commercial complexes, fleet hubs, and highway corridors. Bypasses the vehicle's onboard AC converter for rapid 20–80% top-ups.

Power Output 30 kW – 350 kW DC
20% to 80% Time 25 to 40 minutes
Connector CCS Combo 2 (Combined Charging)
Best Suited For Intercity Highway Transit
