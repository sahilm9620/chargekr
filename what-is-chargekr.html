What Is ChargeKr?

ChargeKr is India's community-powered peer-to-peer EV charging network — built to turn every idle home wallbox and apartment parking socket into an accessible neighbourhood charging point.

How ChargeKr works

Over 90% of residential EV wallboxes and 15A sockets sit unused for 20+ hours a day while their owners are at work or asleep. ChargeKr bridges that gap by connecting KYC-verified EV drivers with local charger hosts:

For EV Drivers: Discover available 15A, Type-2 AC, and CCS2 Fast chargers near your destination, pre-book a slot with zero wait time, and pay seamlessly via UPI.
For Charger Hosts: List your home or society charger in 2 minutes, set your own schedule and hourly rate, and earn ₹5,000–₹15,000/month while keeping 100% of your earnings (₹0 commission).

Who is ChargeKr built for?

ChargeKr serves electric two-wheeler riders (Ather, Ola, TVS iQube, Bajaj Chetak), electric four-wheeler owners (Tata Nexon EV, Punch EV, Tiago EV, MG ZS EV, Mahindra XUV400, BYD, Hyundai Ioniq 5), apartment RWAs, workplaces, and highway travellers across India.
