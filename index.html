FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Common questions about ChargeKr.

What is ChargeKr?
ChargeKr is a community-powered peer-to-peer EV charging network in India. It helps EV owners find and reserve nearby residential, apartment, workplace, and public chargers, and allows charger owners to list their idle chargers and earn passive income.
How much money can I earn by sharing my home EV charger on ChargeKr?
Most residential hosts on ChargeKr earn between ₹5,000 and ₹15,000 per month depending on their location, charger speed (15A socket vs 7.4 kW Type-2 wallbox), and daily available hours. ChargeKr charges ₹0 platform commission, so 100% of the charging income goes directly to the host.
How do I find EV charging stations near me using ChargeKr?
Download the ChargeKr app, allow location access, and filter the live map by your EV's connector type (15A Smart Socket, Type-2 AC, or CCS2 DC Fast). You can compare prices, check real-time availability, reserve a slot in advance with zero waiting queues, and pay automatically via UPI.
Which electric vehicles and connectors are supported on ChargeKr?
ChargeKr supports all electric two-wheelers and four-wheelers in India, including Tata Nexon EV, Punch EV, Tiago EV, Curvv EV, MG ZS EV, Comet EV, Windsor EV, Mahindra XUV400, BYD Atto 3, Hyundai Ioniq 5, Ather 450X, Rizta, Ola S1, TVS iQube, and Bajaj Chetak across 15A, Type-2 AC, and CCS2 DC connectors.
What is the official ChargeKr website and app?
The official ChargeKr website is https://www.chargekr.com/ and the official Android app is available on the Google Play Store (com.chargekr).
