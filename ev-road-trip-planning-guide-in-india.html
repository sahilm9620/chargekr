EV Road Trip Planning Guide in India

Prepare longer journeys with the right charger stops, range planning, and route confidence for EV travel.

Plan your charging stops

For long EV trips, plan charging stops every 120-150 km. Use a mix of DC fast chargers and reliable city chargers to keep your journey smooth.

Use an app to map the route

ChargeKr helps you build a route with available chargers along the way. Choose stops with confirmed availability, especially on highways and in smaller towns.

Charge smart, not full

For road trips, charging from 20-80% is faster than charging to 100%. This saves time and keeps your trip moving without long waits at a charger.

Keep a buffer

Always leave a range buffer for detours and traffic. In India, that extra margin reduces stress and makes it easier to adapt if a charger is temporarily unavailable.
