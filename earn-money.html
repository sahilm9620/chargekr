Regulatory & Metering Guidelines for Hosts

EV charging infrastructure in India operates under Ministry of Power (MoP) guidelines. To ensure full compliance with state electricity regulations:

Commercial & Business Operators: Malls, hotels, commercial parking lots, and fleet depots must maintain an appropriate commercial or dedicated EV-tariff electricity connection from their local DISCOM in accordance with state regulations.
Residential & Community Hosts: Individual residential hosts participate on a private community equipment access and cost-sharing basis (offsetting equipment wear, dedicated parking, and electricity costs). Hosts are responsible for adhering to their local DISCOM supply agreements and apartment society / RWA visitor and parking bylaws.

Disclaimer: ChargeKr is a technology platform connecting EV drivers with independent charger hosts. ChargeKr does not generate, distribute, or resell electricity. All rates shown in calculators and presets are illustrative benchmarks.
