02 / OPERATING CHECKLIST

Essential field guidelines for drivers & hosts.

01

Verify Dedicated Earthing

Indian EVs (especially Tata, MG, and Hyundai) require earth-to-neutral voltage below 5V to initiate charging safely.

HOST
02

Never Use Multi-Plug Extension Cords

Always plug portable 15A EVSE cables directly into a heavy-duty IS 1293 wall socket wired with 2.5 sq.mm+ copper cable.

BOTH
03

Monsoon & Wet Weather Protocol

IP65 wallboxes are safe in heavy rain, but never connect a plug if the parking floor has standing water above ankle depth.

DRIVER
04

Uncoil Charging Cables Completely

Leaving a high-current charging cable tightly coiled during a 3.3 kW or 7.4 kW session traps inductive heat.

DRIVER
05

Keep Connectors Off Driveways

Always return the Type-2 nozzle to its wall holster after unplugging so vehicles never roll over the connector pins.

BOTH
06

Stop Session Before Unplugging

Unlock the vehicle port via your key fob or the ChargeKr app before pulling the handle to prevent arc wear.

DRIVER
03 / SAFETY FAQ

Common electrical questions.

Is it safe to charge an EV outdoors during Indian monsoons?
Yes. Certified Type-2 wallboxes and vehicle inlets are rated IP54 to IP65 against water ingress. Current only flows after the connector is mechanically locked and sealed. Only avoid charging if a basement or stilt bay is actively waterlogged.
Can someone unplug my cable while my car is charging?
No. During an active AC or DC charging session, an electromechanical pin inside your vehicle's charging port locks the Type-2 or CCS2 gun in place until you unlock the car.
Is overnight charging safe for the battery and home wiring?
Yes. Your vehicle's onboard Battery Management System (BMS) continuously regulates cell temperature and automatically cuts off power the moment the battery reaches 100% (or your preset charge limit).
